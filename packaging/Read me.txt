YtLocal - YouTube to MP3/MP4 converter (Windows)
================================================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example your Desktop).
2. Double-click YtLocal.exe.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     (The app isn't signed by a company, so Windows warns about it.)
3. The first time, the Tools panel opens. Click "Download" next to yt-dlp, FFmpeg
   and Deno. This takes a minute, and only happens once.
4. Paste YouTube links, click "Fetch info", pick MP3 or MP4, and click "Download".
   Files go to your Music folder (audio) or Videos folder (video). You can change
   that with "Change...".

Tips
- If downloads start failing, click "yt-dlp ..." at the top right, then "Update yt-dlp".
  YouTube changes often.
- Private playlists can't be downloaded. Set them to Unlisted in YouTube first.

Everything runs on your own PC: no account, no ads, nothing is uploaded.
